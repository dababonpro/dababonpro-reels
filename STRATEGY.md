# DababonPro Content Strategy & Measurement Playbook

Grounded in 2026 short-form algorithm benchmarks for **Facebook Reels**, **YouTube Shorts**, and **TikTok** (AI / developer / faceless tech niche).

---

## 1. Optimal Length by Platform

| Platform | Sweet Spot | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Facebook Reels** | `15–30s` | Fast-scrolling feed; punchy utility wins. |
| **YouTube Shorts** | `22–45s` | A `30s` Short with `85%` retention beats a `60s` Short with `50%`. |
| **TikTok** | `7–15s` (loop) or `30–60s` (story) | High completion rate + rewatches trigger distribution. |

> **Cross-post sweet spot:** `20–30s` works cleanly across all three platforms without re-editing. Never upload watermarked exports (TikTok/CapCut watermarks are suppressed on YouTube Shorts).

---

## 2. Hooks & Titles (First 1–3 Seconds)

- **Swipe-Away Rate** is the #1 filter on Shorts and TikTok. Never start with *"Hey guys"* or slow pans.
- **3 Hook Formulas that convert in AI/Dev:**
  1. **Frustration Hook:** *"Stop fighting broken JSON outputs from LLMs."*
  2. **Utility / Result Hook:** *"Turn any website into clean LLM-ready Markdown."*
  3. **Pattern Interrupt:** Start mid-error (red terminal traceback) or show the finished agent output in frame 1.
- **YouTube Shorts Titles:** Lead with the searchable keyword in `< 60` characters + `#Shorts`.
- **TikTok SEO:** TikTok indexes **on-screen text**, **captions**, and **spoken voiceover**—put the core topic words on screen in the first 3 seconds.

---

## 3. Pacing & Visual Upgrades (Week 2 Roadmap)

- **2–3 Second Visual Shift:** Change visual state every 2–3 seconds (zoom into the README demo/GIF, highlight the `install` command, cut to terminal output) instead of a single continuous scroll.
- **Micro-Hook at 7–10s:** Add a mid-video callout (*"And here's the best part..."* or *"1 command to self-host:"*) to reset attention.
- **Save Triggers:** Explicitly prompt saves (*"Save this repo for your next AI build"*). On dev Reels, **Saves** (`2×–3×` Likes) are the strongest signal.
- **Seamless Loop:** End on a frame or line that flows back into the opening hook.

---

## 4. Music & Audio Rules

- **Music-only Reels:** Dark lo-fi, synthwave, or minimalist tech beats (like `synth_tech_loop.mp3` or Pixabay/TikTok trending audio).
- **Voiceover Explainers (Week 2+):** Keep background music strictly between **`-20dB` and `-25dB`** so technical terms stay crystal clear, and add a subtle `0.0s` digital chime/blip audio hook.

---

## 5. Measurement & Weekly Review

After 7 days of Week 1 data, record these **4 KPIs** per video before tweaking Week 2:

1. **Viewed vs. Swiped Away %** (YouTube Studio → Reach) — Target: **`> 65%` Viewed**.
2. **Average % Viewed / Completion Rate** — Target: **`> 85%`** on `18–30s` videos.
3. **Saves & Shares per 1,000 Views** (Meta Business Suite / TikTok) — Target: **Saves `> 2×` Likes**.
4. **Follower/Subscriber Conversion** — Which format (*Repo Spotlight* vs. *Agent Workflow Demo* vs. *Concept Explainer*) brings actual follows?
