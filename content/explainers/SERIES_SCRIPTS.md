## 1. LLMs — *The Pattern-Guessing Engine*
- **On-screen badge:** `Part 1 of 11 • LLMs`
- **Hook:** How does an LLM actually work?
- **Scene 1 (Analogy):** Think of your phone's autocomplete—except trained on huge amounts of text. Give it *"The cat sat on the..."* and it predicts *"mat."*
- **Scene 2 (Mental Model):** An LLM generates small chunks of text one piece at a time. On its own, it only generates—it takes tools and instructions to turn it into an assistant that can search or act.
- **Scene 3 (The Catch):** A standalone model can't see your private files or today's news unless the app connects tools, so it may guess when it doesn't know.

## 2. RAG — *The Open-Book Test*
- **On-screen badge:** `Part 2 of 11 • RAG`
- **Hook:** Why standalone AI guesses—and how RAG helps.
- **Scene 1 (Analogy):** Taking a hard test from memory forces you to guess when you forget. **RAG** (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) turns it into an **open-book test**.
- **Scene 2 (How it works):** Before answering, the assistant searches your docs or folder, pulls relevant pages, and reads them first.
- **Scene 3 (Honest Limit):** It can answer using retrieved sources—though retrieval can still miss things, so you still need to check important facts.

## 3. MCP — *The USB-C Plug for AI*
- **On-screen badge:** `Part 3 of 11 • MCP`
- **Hook:** What is MCP? Think USB-C for AI tools.
- **Scene 1 (Analogy):** Remember when every gadget needed its own charger? AI apps used to need custom wiring for every calendar, folder, or database.
- **Scene 2 (The Fix):** **MCP** (Model Context Protocol) is a common standard—like a USB-C plug—for connecting AI apps to tools and data.
- **Scene 3 (How it works):** Build an MCP connector once, and compatible AI clients can discover and use those tools without rewriting the integration from scratch.

- **Scene 2 (How it works):** It bundles reusable instructions—your format, tone, and steps—into a file the assistant loads when a matching task comes up.
- **Scene 3 (Honest Limit):** Skills guide the assistant consistently, though they don't guarantee perfection—clear checks still matter.

## 5. Context Engineering — *Packing the Right Backpack*
- **On-screen badge:** `Part 5 of 11 • Context Engineering`
- **Hook:** Why giving AI *more* info can make it *worse*.
- **Scene 1 (Analogy):** Shove fifty textbooks into one backpack and you can't find today's worksheet.
- **Scene 2 (How it works):** **Context Engineering** means packing only what the model needs right now: the key rules, relevant files, and the actual error.
- **Scene 3 (Payoff):** Less clutter keeps the assistant more focused—and often more efficient.
